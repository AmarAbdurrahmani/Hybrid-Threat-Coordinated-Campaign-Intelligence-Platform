What this measures

The score measures coordination, not who is behind it. Infrastructure shows where traffic exits, which for VPNs and proxies is not where the operator sits; state nexus is always "not established" until corroborated by all-source intelligence. No single signal flags an account: in the seed thread, @arben_prishtina posts inside the swarm window and gets a maximum temporal score, but stays Organic because the text, narrative and account history are clean.

Limits

Dialect mixing is normal for diaspora and cross-border writers, so only same-word doublets count. The trope and calque lexicons are hand-written seeds and need extending with fact-checker data. Any score is a lead for human review, not a verdict. All data on this page is simulated.
